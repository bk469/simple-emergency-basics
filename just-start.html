Simple Start

One small step at a time

You don't have to do everything.

Many people feel they should be ready for an emergency. They don't know where to begin, so they do nothing. Then they feel bad about it.

You can let that go. Being ready doesn't happen in one day. It happens one small step at a time.

The 3 Basics

Start with three things. You can do them today.

That's it. If you did these three things, you are more ready than most people. Well done.

Want to Do a Little More?

Try one small step each week. Each one takes about 10 minutes.
